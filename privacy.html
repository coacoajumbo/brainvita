Brainvita Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Brainvita is a peg solitaire game made by KG — Knowledge in Play. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

None. Brainvita does not ask for your email, location, contacts or any other personal information. After a win you may type a player name for the Hall of Fame; it is optional and stays on your device. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things in your phone's own storage: your one-marble wins (date, time taken, number of jumps, and the player name you type in, if any), your best result, which boards and history cards you have unlocked, and your sound, music and marble colour settings. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

Internet and permissions

The game works fully offline. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Children

Brainvita is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any information, because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
