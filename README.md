# Brainvita · KG — Knowledge in Play

Classic 33-hole peg solitaire with glass marbles, a Hall of Fame, history cards and an unlockable French board.
Installs on your phone and works fully offline.

## Install on your phone

Open the game's web address once while online, then:

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Tap the **Install app** banner, or tap the ⋮ menu → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
2. Open Brainvita from your home screen. It runs full-screen and works offline.

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow).
2. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open Brainvita from your home screen. It runs full-screen and works offline.
   If there is no sound, check that the silent switch on the side of the iPhone is off.

## Updating

Edit `brainvita.html` in the parent folder, run `node build-app.js`, then commit and push this folder.
Phones pick up the new version the next time they open the app while online.
